The story

Not just a restaurant.
A reason to linger.

C HOUSE was born from a simple conviction: that Dubai deserved an Italian dining room where time moves differently. Where the pasta is made by hand each morning, where the drinks are built with care, and where the room itself invites you to stay for one more hour.

Tucked into Jebel Ali Recreation Club, we opened our doors with a short menu, a long bar, and a belief that hospitality is about slowing down together. Everything else followed.

How we got here

The journey to
this table.

Every restaurant has a beginning. Ours started with a menu on a single page and a conviction that Dubai had room for something slower.

2022 · Opening

The first table

C HOUSE opened at Jebel Ali Recreation Club with a tightly curated Italian menu, a short drinks list, and twenty tables that were full on night one.

2023 · The Bar

Aperitivo, properly done

We expanded the bar programme with Italian wines, seasonal cocktails, and a shisha terrace that became its own destination. C HOUSE stopped being just dinner.

2024 · Live Nights

Music, every weekend

We introduced live entertainment: jazz sets on Fridays, acoustic sessions on Saturdays. The room found its rhythm and the reservations followed.

Today

Still at the long table

Rated 4.9 on Google, fully booked most evenings, and still making pasta by hand every morning. Not much has changed. Everything has gotten better.
